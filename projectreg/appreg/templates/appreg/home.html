Register to Vote — Make farmers' voices count

A simple, secure registration for farmers and rural communities. Follow a quick set of steps to ensure your voice is included in decisions that affect agriculture and land use.

Begin Registration Learn More
Accessible: Online, mobile-friendly, or assisted on-site.
Secure: Data saved to the national roll and managed by election officials.

How registration works

We kept it simple — three steps for busy farmers.

1. Prepare your documents

Bring a valid ID and proof of residence or a letter from your cooperative/association if required.

2. Fill the form

Complete the registration form online or with staff assistance. Make sure names and dates are accurate.

3. Verify & receive confirmation

Officials will confirm your details; once approved you'll receive confirmation of registration.

Why your vote matters

Support rural livelihoods

Your vote determines policies on land rights, subsidies, rural roads, and market access — things that directly affect harvests and income.

Influence agricultural policy

Voting ensures farmers have a seat at the table when decisions are made about water access, extension services, and crop insurance.

  • Protects community interests and land rights.
  • Improves access to rural services and infrastructure.
  • Gives farmers a stronger voice in local councils.
